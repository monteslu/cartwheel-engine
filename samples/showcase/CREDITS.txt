showcase: asset sources and licenses

CesiumMan.glb (the four walkers)
  Khronos glTF Sample Models, "Cesium Man" by Cesium, licensed CC-BY-4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the license excludes the
  Cesium logo and trademarks (see CesiumMan.LICENSE.md). Taken from
  deps/three.c/test/assets/ and staged into the build's asset directory
  with its license file (CesiumMan.LICENSE.md) by samples/CMakeLists.txt,
  as anim_skinned does. Not modified.

decals/scorch.png, decals/cracks.png
  Generated from code by samples/fx_decals/make-art.mjs (CC0, made in this
  repository); staged from samples/fx_decals/assets/decals/.

Everything else is generated at startup by samples/showcase/world.c and
needs no attribution: the terrain (value-noise heights, vertex colours),
the trees (three.c cone and cylinder geometry), the houses, roofs, lamp
posts, windows and fireflies (primitives), and every light. The sky, the
fog and the post stack are the engine's own. No textures, no sounds.
